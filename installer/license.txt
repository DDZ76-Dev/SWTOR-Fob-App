SWTOR Security Key - unofficial fan-made tool

This app is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Electronic Arts,
Broadsword Online Games or Lucasfilm. It is free, it never asks for your
SWTOR or EA password, and it never connects to the game or EA's servers.

Your security key is stored only on this PC, encrypted for your Windows
account. The only network traffic is time checks against public time servers.

The app starts hidden in the system tray when you sign in to Windows, so it
can appear next to the SWTOR launcher. You can turn this off from the tray
icon menu (Open with SWTOR launcher).
